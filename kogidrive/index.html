NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Important information about this demonstration portal.

Is KogiDrive an official government website?

No. KogiDrive is an academic frontend prototype and is not affiliated with or authorized by the Kogi State Government or any registration authority.

Will I receive a legally valid plate number?

No. The system generates an application reference for demonstration only. It does not allocate official vehicle registration numbers.

Can I access my application on another device?

No. Records are stored in this browser using localStorage. They are not synchronized across devices or browsers.
